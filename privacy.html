Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What this app does

Odoo Backup is an internal tool used only by Odexo Ops. It copies backup files from servers we own to a folder in our own Google Drive account.

Google user data

The app requests only the drive.file permission, which allows it to access files and folders that the app itself creates. It cannot read, modify or delete any other files in Google Drive.

No Google user data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose other than storing our own backup files.

Data storage and removal

Access tokens are stored only on our own servers. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account under Security → Third-party apps.
